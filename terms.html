Terms of Use

Effective date: October 10, 2026

plexserver-rclone is a personal configuration of rclone for its owner's Google Drive account and local media software. It is not a public file-hosting service and does not accept sign-ups.

Owner-controlled operation

The owner is responsible for authorizing account access, selecting files, configuring access and sharing, and managing transfers and deletions. Use must respect applicable laws, file rights, and the terms of the connected services.

Availability and data protection

The integration depends on the owner's equipment, network connection, and third-party services. Availability and uninterrupted playback are not guaranteed. Moving a file to Google Drive and removing its local copy does not create an independent backup. The owner is responsible for maintaining any additional copies needed.

Privacy and questions

The Privacy Policy explains data handling. Contact the maintainer through the project's issue tracker; do not post credentials or private file details in public issues.
